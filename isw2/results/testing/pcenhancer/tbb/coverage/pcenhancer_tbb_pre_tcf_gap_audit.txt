===== 8. SELECTION RULE FOR T_CF =====

Candidate priority must consider:

1. gap magnitude;
2. functional coherence of the uncovered cluster;
3. reachability through a legitimate execution scenario;
4. fixture complexity;
5. expected incremental Line/Branch Coverage;
6. explanatory value of the test.

A method is NOT selected only because it has missed branches.
A small gap is NOT automatically rejected.
The final decision requires manual source/reachability analysis.
