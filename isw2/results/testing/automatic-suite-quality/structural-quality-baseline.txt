Interpretation policy
---------------------
These metrics are descriptive evidence.

They are NOT converted into arbitrary numeric clarity or
maintainability scores.

The following are measured:
- test logic LOC;
- support/scaffolding LOC;
- total LOC;
- number of test methods;
- number of assertion-like statements;
- assertions per test;
- opaque generated test names;
- descriptive test-name percentage.

SonarCloud issue/smell analysis is measured separately.

Comparison rule
---------------
Like-for-like comparisons are performed by technique:

C0 T_RND vs Cx T_RND
C0 T_ES  vs Cx T_ES
C0 T_LLM vs Cx T_LLM
